ERA V5 · Session 3 assignment · 17 Oct 2026. Every number below is either measured in the evidence repository (experiments E1–E5, code and logs) or cited.

Budget40B dense, 20 T tokens (500/weight), 4.8e24 FLOPs ≈ 3.3 M H100-hours.
Indic8% of every batch (always-on): 2% native + 6% translated, plus 4% India-centric English. Native supply is the limit (E4).

1. Pre-training data: what and why

A 40B model at Gemma 4 quality needs ~20 T tokens (Gemma 3 27B: 14 T). Each source's share is capped by its unique supply: share × 20 T ≤ 4 × unique tokens (repeats beyond ~4 epochs lose value; Muennighoff et al., 2023).

Indic supply (E4). Sangraha (verified + unverified, parquet footers) and FineWeb-2 hold 67–111 B unique native Indic tokens under our tokenizer (they overlap; both are Common Crawl). Four epochs of that is 0.27–0.44 T = at most 2.2% of 20 T. A 10% native share would need 18–30 epochs. So the always-on 8% is 2% native (repeated ≤ 4×, growing with new crawls and OCR) + 6% high-value English content translated into the 22 languages. A better tokenizer does not create supply: it carries the same bytes in fewer tokens.

Schedule. Phase 1 (0–16 T): the mix above. Phase 2 anneal (16–19.5 T): up-weight curated, reasoning, agentic and native Indic data. Phase 3 (0.5 T): context 8 K → 128 K with long Indic and code documents. Weights are set by mixture regression on 1B-parameter proxies (RegMix), scored by bits per byte per domain and language.

3. Cleaning for these objectives

  1. Extract (trafilatura / resiliparse); OCR for Indic PDFs; detect legacy fonts (Krutidev, Shree-Lipi) and convert or drop.
  2. Normalize to NFC in the pipeline, not in the tokenizer. E3: 43% of Marathi, 59% of Manipuri, 27% of Konkani documents were not NFC. Keep ZWJ/ZWNJ: they are spelling.
  3. Language + script ID per document (GlotLID / IndicLID incl. romanized) and a script-purity check. E3 found Sangraha verified/kas is mostly mojibake and verified/doi contains English.
  4. Heuristics calibrated per language on its own clean text (FineWeb-2 method): Unicode letters include marks; ।, ॥, ۔ end sentences; stop words from the language's own frequency list.
  5. Quality classifiers per domain and language, grown from seeds by the DeepSeekMath loop (Indian legal, government, educational, each Indic language). Never an English classifier on Indic text.
  6. Dedup: exact + MinHash (word 5-grams) per language after NFC; URL-level for crawls.
  7. PII: Aadhaar (12 digits + Verhoeff check), PAN, +91 mobiles, UPI IDs, emails.
  8. Licence + opt-out filter; code: permissive only.
  9. Decontaminate against every eval set, incl. translated copies; ignore n-grams frequent in training (E5: 13-gram hits were generic phrases).

E2. One BPE trained at 384K and cut to each size (a size-k BPE is the first k merges). The tokenizer's training mix moves Indic cost more than V does.

E2. 40B training FLOPs per byte of the planned mix: (6N + 6dV + attention) × tokens/byte. d = 7,168, 60 layers, 8K context.

Why 262,144. Cost per byte falls 14.5% from 32K to 256K and is flat (±0.7%) from 192K to 384K; 262K is 0.15% above the minimum (320K) and saves 0.8 B embedding weights against it. It is 218 (clean tensor-parallel sharding), equals Gemma 4's V (direct comparison), and at that size our tokenizer spends 32% fewer tokens on tier-1 Indic than Gemma 4 at the same English cost. Two cheaper levers matter more than V: the tokenizer's training mix (English-first mix at the same V: tier-1 2.02, +14% Indic tokens) and the pre-tokenizer (GPT-2's \p{L}+ splits words at every Indic vowel sign: at 64K, Hindi 3.33 vs 1.58 tok/word, Tamil 6.83 vs 2.61). Released tokenizers miss whole script families: BrahmicTokenizer-131K drops Arabic script (Urdu, Kashmiri, Sindhi: 7.4–8.9 tok/word); every o200k-family tokenizer is in byte fallback on Ol Chiki (Santali, 15.3).

6. Risks and next measurements

  • Tokenizer trained on 300 M characters; retrain on ≥ 10 GB of the final mix before the freeze.
  • Confirm with two 1B proxies (ours vs Gemma 4 tokenizer) on equal bytes: bits per byte per language.
  • Native Indic supply is the hard limit: fund new crawls, OCR of government and library archives, and licensed news.
